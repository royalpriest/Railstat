How it works

1

Export your bookings

Save the sheet from your booking system or Excel as .xlsx. Column order doesn't matter.

2

Upload it

Every row is checked. Rows Railstat can't read are set aside and listed, never silently dropped.

3

Read, filter, share

See revenue, cancellations, routes and weekly trends. Print it, save a PDF or export CSV.

What's in the report

Headline numbers first

Net revenue, confirmed tickets, cancellation rate and loyalty share at the top.

A skipped-rows report

Each unreadable row with its Excel row number and the reason.

Charts that compare

Routes busiest first, tickets by class, revenue week by week.

A table you can search

Filter by status, search any field, export what you see.

What your file needs

One sheet with a header row. Headers are matched without caring about case, spaces or order, so “Ticket ID” and “ticketid” both work.
